# ADR NNNN: Short present-tense title naming the decision

## Status

<Accepted | Superseded by [ADR MMMM](MMMM-title.md)>

## Context

<Open with **Affects:** and the sections of `design.md` or `queries.md` this
decision changes, and the tasks in `plan.md` whose done test changes.

Then the forces at work: what the design said, what is true now, and what
changed — a measurement, a platform fact, a dependency, a constraint found in
implementation.

State facts, not conclusions. If a measurement drives this, give the numbers,
the DCS build string, and where the measurement is recorded.>

## Decision

<One paragraph in the active voice, present tense: "The engine reads X." Not
"we should" or "it was decided". Complete enough to act on.>

### Alternatives considered

<Each rejected option and the reason it lost. Omit this heading only when
there was genuinely one option.>

## Consequences

<What becomes true, easier, and harder, including the ones that hurt.

If the decision is provisional, say what would reopen it.>
